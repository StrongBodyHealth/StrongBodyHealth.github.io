Our mission

Personalized Medical Weight Management

StrongBodyHealth provides physician-guided care for patients who may benefit from GLP-1 medications and related weight-management support. Our goal is to make the process clear, individualized, and medically responsible from initial screening through ongoing care.

Meet Dr. Rhonda Hamilton

Dr. Rhonda Hamilton is the physician behind StrongBodyHealth. She provides individualized medical weight-management care and works with patients to determine whether GLP-1 medications may be appropriate as part of a broader treatment plan.

Her approach emphasizes thoughtful evaluation, clear communication, and treatment decisions tailored to each patient’s needs, goals, and medical history.
